<h2 id="explanation">Explanation</h2>

The Schrödinger License is a permissive software license with two possible states: **Unobserved** and **Observed**.

Software begins in the Unobserved State with respect to each person or legal entity. In this state, the software may be used, copied, modified, distributed, sublicensed, and sold without an attribution requirement.

Reading the license constitutes **Observation**.

Once observed, the license irreversibly enters the Observed State with respect to that person or entity. The same permissive rights remain, but copies or substantial portions of the software must include the copyright notice and license.

Observation is independent. One person's observation does not cause the license to become observed for anyone else.

In other words, the license exists in one of two possible states until you check which state applies to you.

Unfortunately, checking requires reading the license.

<h2 id="quick-start">Quick Start</h2>

To use the Schrödinger License for your own project:

1. Copy `LICENSE` into the root of your repository.
2. Replace `<YEAR>` and `<COPYRIGHT HOLDER>` with the appropriate values.
3. Distribute your work under the Schrödinger License.
4. Avoid explaining the contents of `LICENSE` to your users. That would ruin the experiment.

The license text itself is dedicated to the public domain and may be copied, modified, and redistributed freely.
